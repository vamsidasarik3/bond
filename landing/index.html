About the Project

Planned for
Better Living.

Navagruha RRR Prekshitha Enclave is an approved 17-acre residential plotted development near AIIMS Bibinagar along the Hyderabad–Warangal Highway (NH 163). Planned for homeowners and long-term investors, the community provides clearly demarcated plots, wide concrete roads, and pre-installed civic infrastructure for immediate construction.

01

209 Sanctioned Plots

163 to 767 sq. yards with demarcated boundaries, all 100% Vaastu compliant.

02

Ready Infrastructure

30 and 40 foot concrete roads, underground drainage, overhead water tank, and street lighting.

03

Immediate Connectivity

Direct access to NH 163, 2 minutes from Bibinagar Sub Registrar and 5 minutes from AIIMS.
