About the Project

Planned Infrastructure.
Built for Long-term Living.

Navagruha RRR Prekshitha Enclave is an approved 17-acre residential layout located on the NH 163 Warangal Highway corridor near AIIMS Bibinagar. Designed for families and strategic land investors with complete statutory permissions and finished on-ground infrastructure.
